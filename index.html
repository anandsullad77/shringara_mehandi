About Us

The story behind every motif

At Shringara Mehandi, henna is more than decoration — it's a ritual of joy. For over a decade we have adorned brides and families with designs that carry meaning, blessing and beauty.

We use only 100% natural, chemical-free henna prepared in-house for a deep, long-lasting stain that's gentle on your skin.

  • Organic, hand-rolled henna cones
  • Hygienic, on-time service at your venue
  • Designs personalised to your story
